FAQ ① Tools and Online Services
Q. Can I use online services such as VirusTotal, CrackStation, hashes.com, dCode, 
FotoForensics, Aperi'Solve, StegZero, online compilers or tool.lu?
A. Online services that require uploading challenge files are not allowed.
Q. Can I use my personal license for software that requires a commercial license, such as IDA?
A. Because the competition provides the same environment for every team, such tools cannot be 
used this time. Thank you for your understanding.

--- PAGE 22 ---
22
FAQ ② Environment
Q. Windows Defender may detect tools such as Hayabusa/Sigma rules, Metasploit payloads or 
mimikatz. Can they be excluded?
A. Tools that Defender may detect are installed on Kali Linux, so Defender will not detect them.
Q. When are the tool versions decided?
A. Installation starts in mid-October, and the latest version at that time is installed.
